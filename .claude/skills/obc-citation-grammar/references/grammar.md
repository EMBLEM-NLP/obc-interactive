# Grammar reference

## Numbering
Division (A, B, C) → Part (1-12) → Section (x.y) → Subsection (x.y.z) → Article (x.y.z.w) → Sentence ((n)) → Clause ((a)) → Subclause ((i)).
Designators carry a trailing period in headings: `9.32.3.8.` The node id does not: `B/9/9.32.3.8`.

## Definitions
Code: `Absorption trench means an excavation ...` — capitalised term, optional parenthetical qualifier `(Group A)`, then `means`. Act: `"building" means ...` — quoted.
Traps found: `Exit means that part of a means of egress` — the term must not swallow a second `means`. `Certificate for the occupancy of a building described in Sentence 1.3.3.4.(3) of Division C` is a fifteen-word term containing periods; no safe word cap captures it (enumerated residual, `DEF/cavity-wall`).

## Notes
`(See Note A-9.32.3.8.)` in a heading. 44 of 64 such headings have no `note` edge (Track B3): stage7 scans bodies, not headings.

## Hubs
Articles above 50 dependencies (`B/1/1.3.1.2` 677, `B/11/11.5.1.1` 382) are delivered by reference; their own provision text can be the table flattened to prose (56,202 and 126,177 chars). Read the grid, not the prose.
